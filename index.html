About us

Coffee with a story

Welcome to Abol Coffee House. We are named after abol, the first and most celebrated cup of the Ethiopian coffee ceremony: the one that opens the conversation.

Our beans are roasted with care and brewed traditionally, then served with indulgent shakes, fresh juices and a space designed to slow you down. Come for the buna. Stay for the lamps, the greenery and a game of chess.
